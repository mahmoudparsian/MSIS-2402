# Laptop Requirement

[← Course home](../README.md) · **Course information**

> **Bring a fully charged laptop to every class session.**

Every class session ends with that session's **lab**: you start it in the
last ~40 minutes of class, with the instructor available for questions, and
submit it on Camino by **11:00 pm Pacific Time** the same day (see
[`ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md`](ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md) §2–3). You need a
working laptop with you every session.

---

## ☑️ Requirements

- Bring your laptop to **every** class session — fully charged.
- Bring your charger too, in case an outlet is available.
- Install all required software **before** class — see
  [`software_installation/`](../software_installation/README.md).
- Confirm that Python, DuckDB, Marimo, and MySQL all run correctly on your
  laptop before the first lab.

## ⚠️ No Laptop, No Lab

Attendance is mandatory, and the in-class start is part of every lab (see
[`ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md`](ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md) §4). If you arrive
without a charged, working laptop, you cannot start that day's lab in class —
and missing the session's lab means a zero (`0`) on it, worth 70 points.

A dead battery, a forgotten charger, or incorrectly installed software is
**not** an excused reason to miss a lab.

**The one built-in cushion:** the lowest lab score is replaced by the average
of your other nine (see [`ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md`](ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md)
§5, Course Policy #1). It exists to absorb an ordinary, undocumented bad day —
not as a reason to skip preparing. If you have a documented emergency,
illness, or university-approved absence, contact the instructor in advance or
within 48 hours to arrange an alternative (see the *Exception* note in §4).

## 💡 Recommendations

- Charge your laptop the night before class.
- Test your full setup (Python, DuckDB, Marimo, qStudio, MySQL) a few days
  before the first lab — not the morning of.
- Keep a backup copy of your work outside your laptop (for example, cloud
  storage or a USB drive).
