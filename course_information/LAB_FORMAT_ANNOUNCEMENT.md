# Announcement: How the Labs Work

[← Course home](../README.md) · **Course information**

Hello everyone,

The labs are where you practice SQL in this course, and they are **700 of
your 1,000 points**. Here is how they work. Please read this before Session 1.

> **If you read an earlier version of this repository:** the labs now have
> a new format. Lab A is now **finished in class**, Lab B is due at
> **11:59 pm** (not 11:00 pm), every lab has exactly **6 tasks**, and the
> optional **c** labs are gone.

---

## Two labs every session

Every class session has two graded labs. Each lab has **6 tasks**.

| | Lab A | Lab B |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The core lab — practice what you just saw in the lecture and demo | A step more challenging — use the session's SQL on new questions |
| **When you do it** | **In class**, in the last ~40 minutes | **After class** |
| **Deadline** | **Before class ends** | **11:59 pm Pacific Time**, the same day |
| **Points** | **40** — Tasks 1–4: 7 points each; Tasks 5–6: 6 points each | **30** — 5 points per task |
| **Submit on Camino** | assignment **Session N Lab A** | assignment **Session N Lab B** |
| **Late?** | Not possible — the assignment closes when class ends | −3 points for each started 24 hours; more than 72 hours late = 0 |

Each session is worth **70 points** (A + B). There are 10 sessions, so labs
total **700 points**. Your lowest session score is replaced by the average of
your other nine.

## What a session looks like

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| First ~55 minutes | Lecture and live demo |
| Last ~40 minutes | **Lab A** in class — the instructor is there to help |
| By **11:59 pm Pacific Time** | Finish and submit **Lab B** |

## Lab A — in class

- Lab A uses **only** what was taught in that session's lecture and demo.
- When class ends, what you have uploaded is graded. Upload your file
  **before** class ends, even if you have not finished every task.
- Each task is graded on its own: full points, partial points for logic that
  is partly right, or 0. Six good tasks out of six is the goal, but every task
  you finish counts.
- **Extra time on timed tests (OAE):** if the Office of Accessible Education
  has approved extra testing time for you, it applies to Lab A. Send me your
  OAE letter **before Session 1**, and Camino will stay open longer for you
  in every session.

## Lab B — by 11:59 pm

- Lab B is a step more challenging than Lab A.
- You may re-upload until the deadline. Your **last** upload counts.
- Late Lab B: **−3 points for each started 24 hours** after 11:59 pm.
  For example, an upload at 9:00 am the next day loses 3 points. After 72
  hours, Lab B scores 0.

## After the Deadlines

- **Lab A solutions** appear in the session's [`LABS/`](../LABS/) folder after class.
- **Lab B solutions** appear **72 hours after the Lab B deadline**, when the
  late window closes.

## Before Session 1

1. **Bring a fully charged laptop to every session** ([why](LAPTOP.md)).
2. **Install the course software and test it before Session 1**
   ([`software_installation/`](../software_installation/README.md)). Lab A is
   timed, so there is no class time to fix an installation.
3. **Before Session 7**, install MySQL and load the course data (Step 5 of the
   installation guide). Lab A of Sessions 7 and 10 uses it in class, and
   Session 9's Lab B uses it too.
4. Read [How to Submit a Lab](ASSIGNMENTS_and_GRADING.md#3-how-to-submit-a-lab)
   — it shows the exact file names, for example
   `lopez_maria_week1_lab1a_marimo.py`.

## Attendance

Lab A happens in class, so **attendance matters**. If you miss a session, both
of that session's labs (A and B, 70 points) score 0 — unless you have a
documented emergency or an approved absence. In that case, contact me before
the session, or within 48 hours after it. The "lowest lab replaced" rule is
there for one ordinary missed session.
